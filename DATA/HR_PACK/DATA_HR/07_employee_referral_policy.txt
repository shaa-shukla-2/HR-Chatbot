NETWORK INDIA PROPERTY - EMPLOYEE REFERRAL POLICY
(SAMPLE DOCUMENT - FICTIONAL, FOR DEMO USE ONLY)
Document ID: HR-POL-07 | Version 1.0 | Effective: 01 April 2026

1. PURPOSE
1.1 The Company encourages employees to refer qualified candidates for open positions.

2. ELIGIBILITY
2.1 All confirmed employees may refer candidates, except HR team members and the hiring manager for the referred role.
2.2 The candidate must not already be in the Company's recruitment pipeline in the last 6 months.

3. REFERRAL PROCESS
3.1 Referrals must be submitted on the HR portal with the candidate's resume before the candidate applies elsewhere.
3.2 HR will confirm acceptance of the referral within 5 working days.

4. REFERRAL BONUS
4.1 The referral bonus is INR 10,000 for non-sales roles and INR 15,000 for sales roles.
4.2 The bonus is paid after the referred candidate completes 3 months of service.
4.3 The referrer must still be employed on the payment date.
4.4 The bonus is paid in the next salary cycle after eligibility.

5. EXCLUSIONS
5.1 No referral bonus is paid for roles at Director level or above, or for interns.
5.2 Referring family members is allowed, but the referrer cannot take part in the hiring decision.
