NETWORK INDIA PROPERTY - ONBOARDING AND EXIT POLICY
(SAMPLE DOCUMENT - FICTIONAL, FOR DEMO USE ONLY)
Document ID: HR-POL-06 | Version 1.0 | Effective: 01 April 2026

1. ONBOARDING
1.1 New employees must submit ID proof, address proof, educational certificates, PAN, bank details and relieving letters from previous employers on or before the date of joining.
1.2 A laptop or mobile device and CRM access are issued within 3 working days of joining.
1.3 A 1-day induction covers the Company, policies and the CRM system.

2. PROBATION AND CONFIRMATION
2.1 All new employees serve a probation period of 6 months.
2.2 Confirmation is based on a performance review at the end of probation. Probation may be extended by up to 3 months.

3. RESIGNATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
3.1 The notice period is 30 days during probation and 60 days after confirmation.
3.2 Resignation must be submitted in writing to the reporting manager and HR.
3.3 The Company may waive or shorten the notice period at its discretion.
3.4 Leave taken during the notice period does not reduce the notice period unless approved by HR.
3.5 If an employee does not serve the full notice period, the shortfall may be recovered from the Full and Final settlement.

4. EXIT FORMALITIES
4.1 Employees must return the laptop, ID card, access cards, documents and client data.
4.2 Employees must hand over all active client leads and deals in the CRM to the manager.
4.3 No Dues clearance from IT, Accounts and HR is required before the Full and Final settlement.

5. FULL AND FINAL (F&F) SETTLEMENT
5.1 F&F is processed within 45 days of the last working day.
5.2 F&F includes pending salary, eligible incentive (see Payroll Policy, Section 4), leave encashment and other dues.
5.3 Up to 10 days of unused Earned Leave are encashed at exit, calculated on Basic salary.
5.4 Deductions may include notice period shortfall, unreturned assets and unrecovered advances.
5.5 Gratuity is paid as per the Payment of Gratuity Act after 5 years of continuous service.

6. EXPERIENCE AND RELIEVING LETTER
6.1 The experience and relieving letter is issued after F&F clearance.
