NETWORK INDIA PROPERTY - DATA PRIVACY AND CLIENT CONFIDENTIALITY POLICY
(SAMPLE DOCUMENT - FICTIONAL, FOR DEMO USE ONLY)
Document ID: HR-POL-08 | Version 1.0 | Effective: 01 April 2026

1. PURPOSE
1.1 Employees handle sensitive client information, including contact details, financial details, KYC documents and property preferences. This policy sets out how to protect it.

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
2.1 Confidential information includes client data, lead lists, pricing and brokerage terms, developer agreements and employee personal data.

3. RULES FOR HANDLING DATA
3.1 Client data must be stored only in the Company CRM and approved systems.
3.2 Employees must not copy client data to personal phones, personal email or personal cloud storage.
3.3 Employees must not share client data with third parties without written consent from the client and approval from management.
3.4 Client KYC documents must not be shared over personal WhatsApp or other unapproved channels.

4. DEVICE AND ACCESS SECURITY
4.1 Employees must use strong passwords and must not share their CRM or email credentials.
4.2 Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

5. DATA BREACH
5.1 Any suspected data breach must be reported to IT and HR immediately, and no later than 24 hours after discovery.

6. AFTER EXIT
6.1 The confidentiality obligation continues for 2 years after leaving the Company.
6.2 Taking or using client lists or CRM data after exit may lead to legal action.

7. EMPLOYEE PERSONAL DATA
7.1 HR collects employee personal data only for employment purposes and does not share it externally except as required by law.
7.2 Employees may ask HR to correct errors in their own personal data.
