NETWORK INDIA PROPERTY - CODE OF CONDUCT AND POSH POLICY
(SAMPLE DOCUMENT - FICTIONAL, FOR DEMO USE ONLY)
Document ID: HR-POL-03 | Version 1.0 | Effective: 01 April 2026

1. GENERAL CONDUCT
1.1 Employees must act with honesty, integrity and respect toward colleagues, clients and partners.
1.2 Employees must dress professionally. Site staff must carry their company ID card at all times.

2. CONFLICT OF INTEREST
2.1 Employees must not engage in any property deal, brokerage or side business that competes with the Company.
2.2 Employees must disclose any personal interest in a property being sold or marketed by the Company.
2.3 Accepting gifts from clients or developers valued above INR 2,000 is prohibited without written approval from HR.

3. CLIENT AND PROPERTY MISREPRESENTATION
3.1 Employees must not make false claims about a property, price, approvals or possession dates.
3.2 Employees must not collect cash or payments from clients directly outside approved Company channels.

4. PREVENTION OF SEXUAL HARASSMENT (POSH)
4.1 The Company has zero tolerance for sexual harassment at the workplace, including client sites and Company events.
4.2 The Company has an Internal Committee (IC) constituted as per the POSH Act, 2013.
4.3 A complaint may be submitted in writing to the IC within 3 months of the incident. The IC may extend this period for valid reasons.
4.4 The IC conducts an inquiry and keeps the identity of the complainant and respondent confidential.
4.5 The IC completes the inquiry within 90 days.
4.6 This assistant cannot judge or decide the outcome of any complaint. Employees should contact the IC directly at ic@networkindiaproperty.example.

5. DISCIPLINARY ACTION
5.1 Violations of this Code may lead to a warning, suspension, termination or legal action depending on severity.
5.2 Employees will be given an opportunity to explain before disciplinary action is finalised, except in cases of gross misconduct.

6. REPORTING CONCERNS
6.1 Employees can report violations confidentially to HR at hr@networkindiaproperty.example. Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.
