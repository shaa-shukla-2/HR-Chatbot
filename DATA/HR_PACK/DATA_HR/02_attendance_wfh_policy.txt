NETWORK INDIA PROPERTY - ATTENDANCE, WORKING HOURS AND WFH POLICY
(SAMPLE DOCUMENT - FICTIONAL, FOR DEMO USE ONLY)
Document ID: HR-POL-02 | Version 1.0 | Effective: 01 April 2026

1. WORKING HOURS
1.1 Office hours are 10:00 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Saturday (2nd and 4th Saturdays off).
1.2 Sales and site staff follow a flexible schedule based on client appointments but must log attendance daily.

2. ATTENDANCE RECORDING
2.1 Employees must mark attendance daily on the HR portal or the mobile app.
2.2 Site and field staff must mark attendance with location enabled at the first client site visit.
2.3 Three late arrivals (after 10:30 AM) in a month count as one half-day Casual Leave.

3. WORK FROM HOME (WFH)
3.1 Back-office employees (HR, accounts, marketing, IT) may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
3.2 Sales, site and client-facing staff are not eligible for regular WFH.
3.3 WFH requests must be raised on the HR portal by the previous working day.
3.4 Employees on probation are not eligible for WFH.

4. FIELD VISITS AND SITE VISITS
4.1 Field visits to client sites or properties must be logged in the CRM with the client name and property address.
4.2 Conveyance for field visits is governed by the Travel and Conveyance Policy.

5. WEEKEND AND HOLIDAY WORK
5.1 Sales and site staff may be required to work on weekends or public holidays for property viewings, site events or launches.
5.2 Work on a weekly off or public holiday earns a compensatory off (comp-off) equal to the days worked.
5.3 Comp-off must be availed within 60 days of the date worked, otherwise it lapses.
5.4 Comp-off requires prior written approval from the reporting manager.

6. OVERTIME
6.1 No overtime pay is applicable to salaried employees. Extra hours on weekends are compensated through comp-off as per Section 5.
