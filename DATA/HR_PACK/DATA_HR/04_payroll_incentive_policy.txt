NETWORK INDIA PROPERTY - PAYROLL, INCENTIVE AND COMMISSION POLICY
(SAMPLE DOCUMENT - FICTIONAL, FOR DEMO USE ONLY)
Document ID: HR-POL-04 | Version 1.0 | Effective: 01 April 2026

1. SALARY PAYMENT
1.1 Salary is credited on or before the last working day of each month.
1.2 Payslips are available on the HR portal by the 3rd of the following month.
1.3 Statutory deductions include PF, ESI (where applicable), Professional Tax and TDS.

2. SALARY STRUCTURE
2.1 Salary consists of Basic, HRA, Special Allowance and, for eligible roles, a variable component.
2.2 Basic salary is 40% of fixed CTC.

3. SALES INCENTIVE AND COMMISSION
3.1 Sales executives are eligible for incentive on every closed property deal.
3.2 The incentive rate is 0.5% of the deal's agreement value for residential deals and 0.75% for commercial deals, subject to a cap of INR 1,50,000 per deal.
3.3 A deal is considered "closed" only when the agreement is registered and the Company has received the brokerage payment from the developer or seller.
3.4 Incentive is paid in the salary cycle of the month following the month in which the deal is closed.
3.5 If a deal is cancelled after incentive payment, the incentive will be recovered from future payments.
3.6 Team deals are split between executives as recorded in the CRM at the time of booking.

4. INCENTIVE ON RESIGNATION
4.1 An employee who resigns before a deal is closed (as defined in 3.3) is not eligible for the incentive on that deal.
4.2 An employee who resigns after a deal is closed is eligible for the incentive on that deal, paid in the Full and Final settlement.
4.3 Incentive on deals closed during the notice period is payable only if the employee serves the full notice period.

5. ANNUAL INCREMENT AND BONUS
5.1 Performance review is conducted once a year in April.
5.2 Increments, if any, are effective from 1 May and depend on performance and Company results.
5.3 A discretionary annual bonus may be paid and is not a guaranteed part of salary.

6. SALARY QUERIES
6.1 For payroll queries, contact payroll@networkindiaproperty.example.
6.2 Salary details of any employee are confidential. Employees may only access their own payroll information.
