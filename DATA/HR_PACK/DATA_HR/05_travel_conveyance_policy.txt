NETWORK INDIA PROPERTY - TRAVEL AND CONVEYANCE POLICY
(SAMPLE DOCUMENT - FICTIONAL, FOR DEMO USE ONLY)
Document ID: HR-POL-05 | Version 1.0 | Effective: 01 April 2026

1. SCOPE
1.1 This policy applies to business travel, including client site visits and property viewings.

2. LOCAL CONVEYANCE FOR SITE VISITS
2.1 Employees using a personal two-wheeler for client site visits are reimbursed at INR 4 per km.
2.2 Employees using a personal four-wheeler are reimbursed at INR 9 per km.
2.3 Each claim must include the client name, property address and distance travelled, as logged in the CRM.
2.4 Fuel receipts are not required when the per-km rate is claimed.
2.5 Daily local conveyance is capped at INR 800 unless approved by the manager.

3. CAB AND PUBLIC TRANSPORT
3.1 Cab fares for client visits are reimbursable with the receipt if a manager has pre-approved the trip.
3.2 Metro, bus and auto fares are reimbursable with receipts.

4. OUTSTATION TRAVEL
4.1 Outstation travel needs prior written approval from the manager.
4.2 Train travel is by 3AC and flights are economy class.
4.3 Hotel stay is reimbursed up to INR 3,500 per night in metro cities and INR 2,500 in other cities.
4.4 Daily meal allowance for outstation travel is INR 600.

5. CLAIM PROCESS
5.1 Claims must be submitted on the HR portal within 30 days of the expense.
5.2 Claims submitted after 30 days will not be processed.
5.3 Approved claims are paid in the next salary cycle.

6. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
6.1 Fines, traffic challans, personal entertainment and alcohol are not reimbursable.
