NETWORK INDIA PROPERTY - LEAVE POLICY
(SAMPLE DOCUMENT - FICTIONAL, FOR DEMO USE ONLY)
Document ID: HR-POL-01 | Version 1.0 | Effective: 01 April 2026

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
1.1 This policy defines the types of leave available to all confirmed full-time employees of Network India Property (the "Company").
1.2 Employees on probation are eligible for leave as described in Section 8.

2. CASUAL LEAVE (CL)
2.1 Each employee is entitled to 8 days of Casual Leave per calendar year.
2.2 Casual Leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.
2.3 A maximum of 3 consecutive days of Casual Leave may be taken at one time.
2.4 Casual Leave must be applied for at least 1 working day in advance, except in emergencies.

3. SICK LEAVE (SL)
3.1 Each employee is entitled to 10 days of Sick Leave per calendar year.
3.2 A medical certificate is required if Sick Leave exceeds 2 consecutive days.
3.3 Unused Sick Leave cannot be encashed.

4. EARNED / PRIVILEGE LEAVE (EL)
4.1 Employees earn 1.5 days of Earned Leave for every completed month of service (18 days per year).
4.2 A maximum of 30 days of Earned Leave may be accumulated.
4.3 Earned Leave exceeding 30 days lapses at the end of the calendar year.
4.4 Up to 10 days of Earned Leave may be encashed at the time of exit, as per Section 5 of the Onboarding and Exit Policy.
4.5 Earned Leave of more than 5 consecutive days must be applied for at least 15 days in advance.

5. MATERNITY LEAVE
5.1 Eligible female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave, in line with applicable law.
5.2 Maternity leave may begin up to 8 weeks before the expected date of delivery.
5.3 Employees must submit a medical certificate confirming the expected delivery date.

6. PATERNITY LEAVE
6.1 Male employees are entitled to 7 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.

7. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS AND OPTIONAL HOLIDAYS
7.1 The Company observes 12 public holidays per year, published in January.
7.2 Employees may choose 2 optional holidays per year from a published list.
7.3 Employees required to work on a public holiday for property viewings or site events will receive a compensatory off (see Attendance Policy, Section 5).

8. PROBATION
8.1 During the 6-month probation period, employees accrue leave as per this policy but may use only Casual Leave and Sick Leave.
8.2 Earned Leave becomes available for use after confirmation.

9. LOSS OF PAY (LOP)
9.1 Leave taken in excess of available balance will be treated as Loss of Pay.
9.2 Unauthorised absence of more than 3 consecutive days may lead to disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct.

10. HOW TO APPLY
10.1 Leave must be applied for through the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager.
10.2 For queries, contact hr@networkindiaproperty.example.
